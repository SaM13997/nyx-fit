## Design Context

### Users
Nyx Fitness is for hybrid athletes and fitness-focused individuals who balance lifting, conditioning, and body-weight tracking. They often use the app in active contexts like the gym, between sets, or while reviewing progress after training. Their main job to be done is to log workouts and body metrics quickly, monitor progress over time, and stay consistent without friction.

### Brand Personality
The brand should feel bold, sleek, and focused. The interface should evoke confidence first, with a strong secondary sense of motivation. Voice and tone should feel athletic and capable, but never macho, abrasive, or overly gamified.

### Aesthetic Direction
The product is dark-first and mobile-first, with an energetic performance aesthetic already present in the codebase through black surfaces, glassy cards, animated backgrounds, strong contrast, and accent-driven actions. Preserve orange and purple as the primary accent family, using orange for momentum and action and purple for identity, emphasis, and premium depth. Apple Fitness is the closest reference for the overall feel: polished, approachable, refined, and performance-oriented. The UI should not look like a generic SaaS dashboard, a hardcore bodybuilding app, or a soft pastel wellness product.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize fast in-workout usability: large touch targets, clear hierarchy, low-friction flows, and legible data at a glance.
2. Design for focused energy: dark surfaces, strong contrast, and purposeful accent color should create motivation without visual chaos.
3. Keep the product athletic but refined: polished motion, premium typography, and clean composition over aggressive or clichéd fitness tropes.
4. Let progress feel tangible: emphasize trends, streaks, milestones, and performance signals in ways that reinforce confidence.
5. Maintain accessible clarity: meet WCAG AA, avoid relying on color alone, and ensure text, charts, and controls remain readable in real workout conditions.
