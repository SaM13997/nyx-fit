## Design Context

### Users
Nyx Fit serves multiple user types: beginners, intermediate lifters, advanced athletes, and coaches. The product should identify the user's level during profile creation after login, then tailor the experience to that mode's philosophy, defaults, and feature emphasis. The mode must remain switchable in settings. Across all modes, the primary job to optimize is fast workout logging with minimal friction on mobile.

### Brand Personality
Nyx Fit should feel powerful, precise, and playful. The interface should mainly evoke confidence and focus, with room for future mood variations that can later become user-selectable. The tone should feel performance-oriented and modern without becoming macho, noisy, or gimmicky.

### Aesthetic Direction
The current product already establishes a strong dark-first direction with light-mode support, glassy surfaces, bold athletic typography, animated hero treatments, and energetic accent color. Future work should preserve that foundation while refining it toward a cleaner, more deliberate system. Reference points are Apple Fitness for polish and premium motion, and Duolingo for approachable progression and motivation. The product should not look like a generic SaaS dashboard, a hardcore bro-gym app, or a cheap gaming UI. Orange and cyan should remain important accents, while purple can remain dominant when used intentionally rather than indiscriminately.

### Design Principles
1. Optimize every core flow for rapid mobile workout logging before adding visual or feature complexity.
2. Design one shared system that adapts by user mode, so beginners get guidance while advanced users and coaches retain speed and control.
3. Make the product feel confident and performance-driven through clear hierarchy, strong contrast, and deliberate motion rather than visual noise.
4. Keep the dark-first aesthetic premium and athletic, with energetic accents used purposefully and red reserved mainly for destructive states.
5. Meet WCAG AA expectations and support reduced-motion accommodations as a default quality bar.
